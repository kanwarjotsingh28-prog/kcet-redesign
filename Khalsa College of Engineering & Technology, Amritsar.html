Admission

How to apply to KCET, which programs you can join, and who to call when you need help.

Open the official application formCall +91 8198001100

How to apply

  1. Check your eligibilityRequirements differ by program. Confirm them on the official admission page before you apply.
  2. Qualify through the right routeB.Tech seats are generally filled through IKGPTU counselling based on JEE Main merit. Other programs can have their own selection process.
  3. Fill in the online applicationUse the official form and pay the application fee.
  4. Submit your documentsBring originals for verification, then pay the first semester fee to confirm your seat.
This is a general guide. Fees, dates and eligibility change every session, so always confirm them with the admission helpline or the official page.
